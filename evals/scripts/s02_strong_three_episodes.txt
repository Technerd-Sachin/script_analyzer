Title: Don't Marry Him

EPISODE 1: The Message

INT. METRO CARRIAGE - NIGHT
ON SCREEN: Text from unknown number: "Don't marry him tomorrow. He killed your sister."
ANAYA (27), bridal mehendi still drying on her palms, reads it twice. The carriage empties around her.
ANAYA: (typing) Who is this?
ON SCREEN: Unknown number: "Ask Dev where he was on 14 March."
Her hands shake. Her younger sister Kiara fell from a terrace on 14 March last year. The police called it an accident.
CUT TO:

INT. DEV'S CAR - NIGHT
Rain on the windshield. DEV (30), easy smile, drives her home.
ANAYA: Where were you on the fourteenth of March?
DEV: (laughing) Is this a wedding quiz?
ANAYA: Answer me, Dev.
DEV: Bangalore. A client meeting. You saw the photos.
ANAYA: Photos can be faked.
Dev pulls over. The smile is gone.
DEV: Who has been talking to you?
ANAYA: Nobody.
DEV: Then why are you gripping that door like you want to jump?
She says nothing. He unlocks the doors.
DEV: Go. I'll be at the mandap at nine. Will you?

INT. ANAYA'S ROOM - NIGHT
Anaya dials the unknown number. A ringtone answers... from inside her own wardrobe.
She yanks it open. Wrapped in a yellow dupatta, a cracked phone glows.
ANAYA: (whispering) This is Kiara's phone. It went missing the night she fell.

EPISODE 2: The Terrace

EXT. KIARA'S OLD BUILDING, TERRACE - NIGHT
Wind. A broken railing, still wrapped in police tape. Anaya scrolls through the cracked phone.
ON SCREEN: Kiara's last chat, to a contact saved as "D": "I know what you did. Terrace. 11 pm."
ANAYA: D. Dev.
Footsteps behind her. She spins. Dev, soaked and breathless.
DEV: I followed you. Give me that phone.
ANAYA: So it's true.
DEV: It's not what you think.
ANAYA: Then tell me what to think! She texted D, and an hour later she was dead.
DEV: I'm not D. I swear on my mother.
He steps closer. She steps back, toward the broken railing.
DEV: Anaya, stop. Not there. Please.
ANAYA: Why do you care?
DEV: Because I've been hunting Kiara's killer for a year. That's why I walked into your life.
(beat)
ANAYA: You met me... on purpose?
DEV: At first. Then I fell in love with you. That part was never the plan.
She wants to believe him. She almost does.
Behind the water tank, a woman steps out of the shadows. A face Anaya knows better than her own.
KIARA: Hello, Didi. You came. Good.

EPISODE 3: The Signature

EXT. KIARA'S OLD BUILDING, TERRACE - CONTINUOUS
ANAYA: Kiara? You're... alive?
KIARA: Alive enough to stop your wedding.
DEV: (stunned) You let us mourn you. For a whole year.
KIARA: Someone pushed me, Dev. I caught the drainpipe. I let them think I fell.
ANAYA: Who pushed you? Say it.
KIARA: Didi, who did I save as D when I was six?
Anaya goes still.
ANAYA: Daddy.
KIARA: Papa was washing money through Dev's family firm. I found the ledgers. I texted him to meet me here.
DEV: (to Anaya) That's why my father's partner vanished. The trail ended at your house.
ANAYA: No. Papa carried you on his shoulders. Papa cried for a month.
KIARA: He cried because he thought I was dead. Not because he was sorry.
Kiara holds out her phone.
ON SCREEN: Photo of a cheque, signed "R. K. Malhotra".
Anaya's knees give way. Dev catches her.
DEV: We go to the police tonight. Together.
A car door slams below. Heavy footsteps on the stairs.
MALHOTRA (60), Anaya's father, still in his kurta from the sangeet, steps onto the terrace, breathing hard.
MALHOTRA: Anaya, come here. Step away from her.
ANAYA: Papa, it's Kiara. She's alive.
MALHOTRA: (quietly) I identified Kiara's body myself, beta. That girl is not Kiara.
