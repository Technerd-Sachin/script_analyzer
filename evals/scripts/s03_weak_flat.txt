Title: The First Day

INT. SHARMA HOUSE, KITCHEN - MORNING
It is a normal morning in the Sharma house. NEHA (24) sits at the table. Her mother SUNITA (50) makes tea.
SUNITA: Good morning, Neha. Did you sleep well last night?
NEHA: Good morning, Mummy. Yes, I slept well. Did you sleep well?
SUNITA: Yes, I also slept well. The weather is very nice today.
NEHA: Yes, the weather is nice. Is there any more toast?
SUNITA: Yes, there is more toast. Here is your toast.
NEHA: Thank you, Mummy. This toast is very good.
SUNITA: You are welcome. So what are you doing today?
NEHA: Today is my first day at my new job at the bank. I am a little nervous.
SUNITA: Do not be nervous. I am proud of you.

INT. BANK OFFICE - DAY
Neha enters a clean office. Her manager KAPOOR (45) meets her.
KAPOOR: Hello, Neha. I am Mr. Kapoor, your manager. This is your desk. Lunch is at one o'clock.
ROHAN (26) looks at her from the next desk. He does not smile.
ROHAN: So you are the new girl who got the job.
NEHA: Yes, I am Neha. Is something wrong?
ROHAN: Yes. I am angry because I applied for this job too and I did not get it.
NEHA: I understand that you are angry. I am sorry that you did not get the job.
ROHAN: It is okay. It is not your fault. I am not angry anymore. Let us be friends.
NEHA: Yes, let us be friends.

INT. SHARMA HOUSE, DINING ROOM - NIGHT
The family eats dinner together.
SUNITA: How was your first day, Neha?
NEHA: It was a good day, Mummy. I made a new friend. His name is Rohan.
SUNITA: That is very good. I am happy for you.
NEHA: Thank you. I think I will like my new job.
They smile and continue eating.
