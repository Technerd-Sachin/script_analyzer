Title: The Missing Money

INT. GUPTA GENERAL STORE - NIGHT
POOJA (22) counts the day's cash for the third time. Her face drains.
POOJA: Twenty thousand short. Again.
Her father GUPTA (58) pulls the shutter halfway down.
GUPTA: Count it again, beta. Your eyes are tired.
POOJA: Papa, this is the third week in a row.
GUPTA: Only three people have the drawer key. You, me and Sameer.
Pooja looks away.
GUPTA: Say it. You think your brother is stealing from me.
POOJA: I didn't say that.
GUPTA: You didn't have to.

INT. GUPTA HOUSE, SAMEER'S ROOM - NIGHT
Pooja slips in and unzips Sameer's college bag.
Inside are bundles of cash, still in the store's rubber bands, and a betting slip.
SAMEER (19) stands in the doorway.
SAMEER: Put it back, Didi.
POOJA: Betting? Papa skipped his medicines to pay the rent!
SAMEER: I was going to return it. I was winning.
POOJA: Nobody wins, Sameer. Who are these people?
SAMEER: If I don't pay them by Friday, they'll come to the store.
Footsteps on the stairs. Gupta stands in the doorway. He has heard everything.
GUPTA: Whose money is that?
(beat)
SAMEER: Yours, Papa. I took it. I'm sorry.
Gupta is silent for a long moment. Then he sits down on the bed.
GUPTA: I am not angry, beta. I am only sad that you did not come to me first.
SAMEER: I was scared. I promise I will never bet again.
GUPTA: I believe you. We will pay them together, and you will work in the store.
SAMEER: Thank you, Papa. Thank you, Didi.
POOJA: Just never lie to us again.
They hug. Gupta laughs and wipes his eyes.
GUPTA: Now, who is hungry? Your mother made kheer.
The family sits down to eat together. Everything is fine again.
