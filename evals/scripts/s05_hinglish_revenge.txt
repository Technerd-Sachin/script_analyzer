Title: Kavya Ka Badla

EPISODE 1: Ulti Ginti

INT. KAVYA'S ROOM, CHAWL - NIGHT
ON SCREEN: Newspaper cutting: "Builder Mohan Saxena jailed for land fraud. Dies of heart attack in custody."
KAVYA (26) pins the cutting on a wall covered with photos. In the centre is VIKRAM RATHORE. A red circle around his face.
KAVYA: Paanch saal, Rathore sahab. Paanch saal maine wait kiya. Aaj se tumhari ulti ginti shuru.
Her mother SUDHA (50) stands at the door with a plate of rotis.
SUDHA: Kavya, yeh sab chhod de beta. Woh log bahut bade hain.
KAVYA: Isliye toh, Maa. Jitna bada aadmi, utna bada girna.
SUDHA: Tere papa yeh kabhi nahi chahte the.
KAVYA: Papa jail mein mar gaye, Maa. Unhone kya chaha, kisi ne poocha?
Sudha has no answer. She puts the plate down and leaves.
CUT TO:

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE, ADITYA'S CABIN - DAY
Glass walls. City view. ADITYA RATHORE (29), sleeves rolled up, flips through Kavya's resume.
ADITYA: Kavya Mehra. MBA, gold medal. Phir bhi PA ki job? Kyun?
KAVYA: Kyunki main seekhna chahti hoon, sir. Aur Rathore Group se accha school kaunsa hai?
ADITYA: (smiling) Flattery se job nahi milti, Miss Mehra.
KAVYA: Toh mera kaam dekh lijiye. Ek hafta. Pasand na aaye toh nikaal dena.
Aditya studies her. Something about her unsettles him.
ADITYA: Monday se aa jao.
Kavya walks out. Her smile drops the moment the door closes.

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE, VIKRAM'S CABIN - DAY
VIKRAM RATHORE (58) watches Kavya on a CCTV screen. His right-hand man SHEKHAR (40) stands beside him.
VIKRAM: Yeh nayi ladki kaun hai?
SHEKHAR: Aditya baba ki nayi PA. Kavya Mehra.
Vikram zooms in on her face. He goes very still.
VIKRAM: Mehra? Nahi, Shekhar. Yeh chehra maine pehle dekha hai. Pata karo yeh asli mein kaun hai.

EPISODE 2: Locker Number Saat

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE - DAY
Kavya works fast. Files, meetings, calls. Aditya notices everything.
ADITYA: Tumne teen din mein woh kar diya jo meri pichli PA teen mahine mein nahi kar payi.
KAVYA: Aapne pichli PA ko kabhi thank you bola tha?
ADITYA: (laughing) Accha. Thank you, Miss Mehra.
KAVYA: Welcome, sir.
A moment between them. She breaks it first.

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE, PANTRY - DAY
Kavya pours tea. Through the half-open door, she hears Shekhar on the phone.
SHEKHAR: (low) Haan, Saxena wali file locker number saat mein hai. Sahab ke study mein. Koi touch nahi karega.
Kavya's cup trembles. Saxena. Her father's name.

INT. RATHORE MANSION, PARTY HALL - NIGHT
Vikram's birthday party. Chandeliers, music, a hundred guests. Kavya arrives with Aditya, in a borrowed saree.
VIKRAM: Toh aap hain Kavya. Aditya aapki bahut tareef karta hai.
KAVYA: Aapke bete ka dil bada hai, Rathore sahab.
VIKRAM: Aur aapka? Aapka dil kitna bada hai?
KAVYA: Utna, jitni zaroorat ho.
Vikram smiles. His eyes do not.

INT. RATHORE MANSION, VIKRAM'S STUDY - NIGHT
Dark. Kavya slips in. She finds a steel locker marked 7. She works a hairpin into the lock. Click.
Inside is a red file marked "SAXENA LAND DEAL". Her father's signature, written in someone else's hand.
KAVYA: (whispering) Papa ne sign kiya hi nahi tha...
The lights snap on.
ADITYA: Kavya? Tum Papa ke study mein kya kar rahi ho?

EPISODE 3: Jhooth

INT. RATHORE MANSION, VIKRAM'S STUDY - CONTINUOUS
Kavya slides the red file behind her back.
KAVYA: Main... washroom dhoondh rahi thi. Yeh ghar toh bhool-bhulaiya hai.
ADITYA: Locker ke andar washroom?
KAVYA: Locker khula tha, sir. Maine bas band kiya.
Aditya looks at the locker. Then at her. He decides to believe her.
ADITYA: Papa ko pata chala toh hum dono ki class lag jayegi. Chalo, bahar.

EXT. RATHORE MANSION, GARDEN - NIGHT
Fairy lights. The party noise is far away. Aditya hands her a glass of juice.
ADITYA: Sach bataun? Mujhe yeh parties pasand nahi hain. Sab log Papa ko khush karne aate hain.
KAVYA: Aur aap?
ADITYA: Main bhi. Bachpan se.
KAVYA: Kabhi unke against gaye ho?
ADITYA: Ek baar. Paanch saal pehle. Unhone ek builder ko jail bhijwaya tha. Mujhe laga kuch galat hai.
Kavya stops breathing.
KAVYA: Phir?
ADITYA: Phir Papa ne kaha, business mein sawal mat poochho. Aur maine nahi poocha.
KAVYA: (softly) Kabhi kabhi sawal na poochhna bhi gunah hota hai.
Aditya looks at her for a long time.
ADITYA: Tum baaki sab se alag ho, Kavya.
She looks away. There is guilt in her eyes.

INT. KAVYA'S ROOM, CHAWL - NIGHT
Kavya hides the red file under her mattress. Sudha is asleep. Kavya lies down and stares at the ceiling fan.
KAVYA: (to herself) Bahut thak gayi hoon. Kal dekhte hain.
She switches off the light and goes to sleep.

EPISODE 4: Pehchaan

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE, LOBBY - DAY
Kavya swipes her access card. Red light. Denied. Shekhar waits by the gate.
SHEKHAR: Sahab ne bulaya hai. Abhi.

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE, VIKRAM'S CABIN - DAY
Vikram sits behind his desk. On it lies Kavya's old college ID. The name on it is Kavya Saxena.
VIKRAM: Baitho, Kavya Saxena.
Kavya does not sit.
KAVYA: Toh aapko pata chal gaya.
VIKRAM: Pehle din se. Tumhari aankhein bilkul tumhare baap jaisi hain. Ziddi.
KAVYA: Aapne mere papa ko fasaaya. Unke signature forge karke.
VIKRAM: Mujhe chor bol rahi ho? Mere hi office mein, mere bete ki jhoothi PA?
KAVYA: Ulta chor kotwal ko daante.
VIKRAM: Saboot hai tumhare paas?
KAVYA: Locker number saat ki red file.
Vikram laughs. He opens a drawer and drops the red file on the desk.
VIKRAM: Yeh wali? Tumhare mattress ke neeche se mili. Tumhari Maa ne Shekhar ko bahut acchi chai pilayi.
Kavya's blood runs cold.
KAVYA: Maa kahan hai?
VIKRAM: Safe hai. Abhi tak. Aaj raat sheher chhod do.
Kavya grabs the desk phone. The line is dead.

INT. RATHORE GROUP OFFICE, CORRIDOR - DAY
Kavya runs and crashes straight into Aditya.
ADITYA: Kavya? Kya hua? Tum ro kyun rahi ho?
KAVYA: Tumhare papa ne meri Maa ko utha liya hai.
ADITYA: Kya bakwaas hai yeh?
KAVYA: Mera naam Kavya Mehra nahi hai, Aditya. Main Mohan Saxena ki beti hoon.
Aditya steps back as if she has slapped him. Her phone buzzes.
ON SCREEN: Message from Maa's number: "Ek ghante mein Rathore farmhouse. Akeli aana. Police ko bataya toh Maa gayi."

EPISODE 5: Hisaab

EXT. RATHORE FARMHOUSE - NIGHT
Floodlights. Kavya walks in alone. Sudha sits tied to a chair, frightened but unhurt. Vikram and Shekhar wait.
VIKRAM: Smart ladki ho. Akeli aayi.
KAVYA: Maa ko jaane do. Mere saath jo karna hai, karo.
VIKRAM: Tum mujhe kya de sakti ho, Kavya? Tumhare paas kuch nahi bacha.
KAVYA: Bas ek sawal ka jawab. Phir main sheher chhod dungi. Papa hi kyun?
VIKRAM: Kyunki tumhara baap zameen bechne ko taiyaar nahi tha. Aur mujhe na sunne ki aadat nahi hai.
KAVYA: Isliye unke signature forge kiye. Unhe jail bhijwaya.
VIKRAM: Aur jail mein woh heart attack... woh bhi maine arrange kiya tha. Khush?
Silence. Then a voice from the dark.
ADITYA: Bahut khush, Papa.
Aditya steps into the light, his phone raised. Recording.
VIKRAM: Aditya! Yeh ladki tumhe use kar rahi hai!
ADITYA: Shayad. Lekin sach aapne khud bola. Live. Commissioner sahab sun rahe hain.
Sirens rise in the distance. Shekhar runs for the car. Police jeeps block the gate.
Vikram sinks into a chair, staring at his son.
VIKRAM: Apne hi baap ke saath yeh?
ADITYA: Aapne kisi ke baap ke saath kya kiya tha?
Police lead Vikram away. Kavya unties Sudha. They hold each other and cry.

EXT. RATHORE FARMHOUSE, GATE - NIGHT
Red and blue lights flash across Aditya's and Kavya's faces.
ADITYA: Tumne mujhse jhooth bola. Pehle din se.
KAVYA: Haan. Aur tumne phir bhi mera saath diya. Kyun?
ADITYA: Kyunki kabhi kabhi sawal na poochhna bhi gunah hota hai. Tumne hi kaha tha.
She almost smiles. Her phone rings. Unknown number. She answers.
MOHAN (V.O.): Kavya... beta, main hoon. Tera papa.
The phone slips from her fingers. Sudha turns, white as a sheet.
MOHAN (V.O.): Main zinda hoon. Aur Vikram akela nahi tha.
