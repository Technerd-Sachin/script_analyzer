Title: The Pinecrest

EPISODE 1: Room 214

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - NIGHT
Snow against the windows. A grandfather clock strikes eleven. ISHA (28) drags a suitcase across the marble floor. The front desk is empty.
ISHA: Hello? Anyone?
KABIR (31), the hotel manager, steps out of the back office. He freezes when he sees her.
KABIR: Isha.
ISHA: Ten years, Kabir. You could at least say welcome home.
KABIR: Welcome home. I'm sorry it took a funeral.
ISHA: Where did he die?
KABIR: Room 214. The doctor said it was his heart.
ISHA: Dad climbed Jakhu hill every morning. His heart was fine.
Kabir looks away. Behind him, the clock ticks.
KABIR: Get some sleep. The will is read tomorrow.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, DINING HALL - DAY
A long table, cold tea. MAYA (52), Isha's stepmother, in white silk. NIKHIL (25), Isha's half-brother, bouncing his knee. Kabir stands by the door with a sealed envelope.
KABIR: Mr. Sood made me executor. I'll keep it short.
He opens the envelope and reads.
KABIR: "The Pinecrest Hotel, with its land and buildings, goes to my daughter Isha."
NIKHIL: All of it? I'm his son too!
MAYA: Nikhil. Sit down.
NIKHIL: She left! She didn't even call him on his birthday!
ISHA: I didn't ask for this.
MAYA: Then don't keep it. A Chandigarh developer offered sixty crore. Sign it to me, and we split it three ways.
ISHA: Dad turned down every developer for thirty years.
MAYA: Your father isn't here, Isha. We are.
Maya leaves. Nikhil follows and slams the door.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, ROOM 214 - NIGHT
Dad's reading glasses are still on the desk. His shawl is folded on the bed. Isha sits in his chair and finally lets herself cry.
She runs her fingers along his bookshelf. One book is too light. She opens it. It is hollow.
Inside is a folded page in her father's handwriting.
ON SCREEN: Note in Dad's handwriting: "Isha, if I die suddenly, it was not my heart. Trust no one at the Pinecrest. Not even K."
ISHA: (whispering) K...
A knock at the door. Three soft taps.
KABIR (O.S.): Isha? Are you awake? I need to tell you something.
Isha looks from the door to the note. Not even K.

EPISODE 2: The Offer

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - MORNING
Isha comes down with dark circles under her eyes. Kabir waits at the desk.
KABIR: You didn't open the door last night.
ISHA: I was asleep. What did you want to tell me?
KABIR: (hesitating) Only that the staff is with you. Whatever you decide.
ISHA: That's it?
KABIR: That's it.
She doesn't believe him. The front doors open. Cold air. INSPECTOR RAWAT (45), polished boots, warm smile, takes off his cap.
RAWAT: Miss Sood. My condolences. Your father was a friend to the whole district.
ISHA: Inspector. I want a post-mortem.
RAWAT: (gently) The cremation was done the same evening, beta. There is nothing left to examine.
ISHA: The same evening? Who signed for that?
RAWAT: Your stepmother. She wanted to avoid a scandal before the tourist season.
ISHA: He died of a heart attack. Where is the scandal in that?
Rawat smiles and puts his cap back on.
RAWAT: Grief makes us see ghosts. I'll close the file today. Call me if you need anything.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, MAYA'S SUITE - DAY
Maya applies lipstick at a mirror. Isha storms in.
ISHA: You cremated him before anyone could look at him.
MAYA: I said goodbye to my husband the way our family always has.
ISHA: In six hours?
MAYA: Rawat said a post-mortem would bring reporters. Reporters would kill the sale.
ISHA: So it's about the sale.
MAYA: It's about survival. Your father left debts you know nothing about. The offer expires on Friday.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, BAR - NIGHT
Nikhil slumps over the counter, refreshing a betting app. Isha sits beside him.
NIKHIL: Come to gloat?
ISHA: Where were you the night Dad died?
NIKHIL: In my room. Why?
ISHA: Who do you owe money to?
Nikhil turns his phone face down.
NIKHIL: Nobody you need to know.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, SECURITY ROOM - NIGHT
Isha scrolls through the CCTV archive. Every camera, every night. Except one.
ON SCREEN: Corridor 2 camera, 14 November: "Footage deleted 03:12. User: KABIR."
Isha stares at the screen. Behind her, the door creaks open.

EPISODE 3: The Footage

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, SECURITY ROOM - CONTINUOUS
Kabir stands in the doorway.
KABIR: I can explain.
ISHA: You deleted the corridor footage from the night my father died. Explain that.
KABIR: It showed Nikhil. Outside Room 214. At two in the morning.
ISHA: Nikhil?
KABIR: Your father loved that boy. If the police saw it, they'd pin it on him. So I deleted it.
ISHA: You had no right.
KABIR: Your father asked me to protect this family. I did what I thought he'd want.
ISHA: Dad left a note, Kabir. It says not to trust K.
Kabir's face goes pale.
KABIR: And you think K is me.
ISHA: Give me one reason it isn't.
KABIR: I can't. I can only tell you I loved him too.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, NIKHIL'S ROOM - NIGHT
Nikhil throws clothes into a bag. Isha blocks the door.
ISHA: You were outside his room at two in the morning.
NIKHIL: (cracking) I went to beg him for money. I owe fifteen lakh to some men in Chandigarh.
ISHA: Did you go in?
NIKHIL: No! I heard him talking to someone. A man. Calm voice. Deep. They were arguing about papers.
ISHA: Who was it?
NIKHIL: I don't know. I got scared and left. In the morning... he was gone.
ISHA: Does your mother know about the money?
NIKHIL: She knows I owe something. She doesn't know how much.
He sits on the bed and sobs. After a moment, Isha sits beside him.
ISHA: Don't run. Running makes you look guilty.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, BACK OFFICE - NIGHT
Past midnight. A thin light under the office door. Isha pushes it open.
ZOYA (24), the new receptionist, is photographing pages of the old land ledger with her phone. She spins around.
ISHA: Zoya? What are you doing with my father's ledger?
ZOYA: Please close the door.
ISHA: I'm calling the police.
ZOYA: Don't. Not the police. Please.
Isha hesitates, her thumb over the call button.
ZOYA: I'm not a receptionist, Isha. And I'm the reason your father is dead.

EPISODE 4: The Source

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, BACK OFFICE - CONTINUOUS
ZOYA: I'm a reporter. I write for a news site in Delhi. Your father was my source.
ISHA: Source for what?
ZOYA: The forest land around this hotel. Someone has been forging old land records and selling protected forest to builders.
ISHA: And Dad found out.
ZOYA: He found the original registers. They prove the forgery. He agreed to give them to me on the fifteenth of November.
ISHA: He died on the fourteenth.
ZOYA: (quietly) I pushed him to talk. If I hadn't, he'd still be alive.
Isha sinks into a chair.
ISHA: Where are the registers now?
ZOYA: That's what I've been looking for. These pages are copies. The originals are hidden somewhere in this hotel.
ISHA: Why should I trust you?
Zoya shows her a chat on her phone.
ON SCREEN: Message from Vijay Sood, 13 November: "Zoya, come on the 15th. I'll bring everything. Tell no one, not even the police."
ISHA: Not even the police...
ZOYA: He was scared of someone in uniform. He never said who.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, KITCHEN - NIGHT
Kabir grates ginger into a pan of boiling tea. Isha watches him from the doorway.
KABIR: Your father's recipe. Ginger, two cardamoms, no sugar. He drank it every night at eleven.
ISHA: Who else knew that?
KABIR: Everyone who ever sat with him. Half of Shimla.
He pours her a cup. She doesn't drink it.
KABIR: You still don't trust me.
ISHA: I don't trust anyone in this hotel. Dad told me not to.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - DAY
Morning. Guests check out. Inspector Rawat walks in with two constables. His smile is gone.
RAWAT: Miss Sood. You wanted a post-mortem. You were right.
ISHA: What?
RAWAT: The hospital kept a blood sample from your father. The lab report came this morning. Poison.
Maya, coming down the stairs, grips the railing.
MAYA: Poison? In this house?
RAWAT: We searched the staff lockers an hour ago.
He holds up a small brown bottle in an evidence bag.
RAWAT: This was in your manager's locker. Kabir's.
Kabir steps out of the office. The constables take his arms.
KABIR: Isha, I didn't. You know I didn't.
Isha looks at him. The note burns in her pocket. Not even K.
RAWAT: Kabir, you are under arrest for the murder of Vijay Sood.

EPISODE 5: The Clock

INT. SHIMLA POLICE STATION, LOCKUP - DAY
Kabir sits on a steel bench, his lip split. Isha stands outside the bars.
ISHA: Did they hit you?
KABIR: It doesn't matter. Listen to me. I didn't just delete the footage.
ISHA: What do you mean?
KABIR: I copied it first. To a pen drive. In case Nikhil ever needed proof he didn't go inside.
ISHA: Did you watch it?
KABIR: Only the minute with Nikhil. Then I panicked and deleted the rest.
ISHA: Where is the pen drive?
KABIR: Inside the grandfather clock in the lobby. Behind the pendulum.
ISHA: Why didn't you tell me before?
KABIR: Because you looked at me like I killed him.
A constable rattles his keys at the end of the corridor.
KABIR: (low) And Isha... don't tell Rawat about the pen drive.
ISHA: Why not?
KABIR: Because I never kept anything in my locker. Somebody put that bottle there.

INT. SHIMLA POLICE STATION, CORRIDOR - DAY
Isha walks out. Rawat leans against the wall, sipping tea from a steel glass.
RAWAT: Visiting a murderer, Miss Sood?
ISHA: Visiting a friend.
RAWAT: Your father said the same thing about people. It never ended well for him.
ISHA: What did he say about you?
RAWAT: (smiling) That I was the only man in Shimla he trusted.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - NIGHT
The lobby is empty. Isha opens the glass door of the grandfather clock and reaches behind the pendulum.
Dust. Cobwebs. Nothing else. The pen drive is gone.
ISHA: (to herself) Who else knew?
Zoya comes in with two cups of tea.
ZOYA: Found it?
ISHA: Someone got here first.
ZOYA: Then someone is listening to everything we say.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, UPSTAIRS CORRIDOR - NIGHT
Isha walks back to her room. Maya's door is open a crack. Maya is on the phone, low and tense.
MAYA: (on phone) I told you. She'll sign by Friday.
(beat)
MAYA: (on phone) And if she doesn't, she ends up like him. I know. I know!
Isha presses herself against the wall, her hand over her mouth.

EPISODE 6: Friday

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, MAYA'S SUITE - NIGHT
Isha walks in without knocking. Maya drops her phone.
ISHA: She ends up like him. That's me, isn't it?
MAYA: You were listening at my door?
ISHA: Who were you talking to?
MAYA: The men Nikhil owes money to. They called me, not you.
ISHA: And "ends up like him"?
MAYA: Those were their words, Isha! Not mine. They want the hotel sold so I can pay them. Or they hurt my son.
ISHA: Why didn't you go to the police?
MAYA: I did. I went to Rawat the day your father died.
ISHA: And?
MAYA: He said a post-mortem would scare the buyer. Cremate quickly, he said, and the debts go away.
Isha goes very still.
ISHA: Rawat told you to cremate him?
MAYA: He was only trying to help.
ISHA: Or he was making sure nobody tested Dad's blood.
Maya sits down slowly on the bed.
MAYA: Then why would he test it now?
ISHA: To put it on Kabir.
For the first time, Maya looks frightened of something other than money.
MAYA: Where is Nikhil?

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, NIKHIL'S ROOM - NIGHT
The room is empty. The window is open. Snow blows in. Nikhil's phone lies on the floor, its screen cracked.
MAYA: Nikhil? Nikhil!
ISHA: He was here an hour ago. I heard his music.
Isha picks up the phone. It buzzes in her hand.
ON SCREEN: Message from unknown number: "Your brother is with us. Sign the sale papers by Friday noon. No police."
A photo loads below it. Nikhil, tied to a chair, a bruise on his cheek.
Maya screams.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - NIGHT
Zoya is at the front desk. Isha and Maya rush down the stairs.
ZOYA: What happened?
ISHA: They took Nikhil.
ZOYA: Who?
Isha's phone buzzes again.
ON SCREEN: Unknown number: "Tell the reporter girl to stay out of it. We know who she is."

EPISODE 7: The Badge

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, DINING HALL - DAY
Isha signs a stack of sale papers. Maya watches, hollow-eyed. Zoya paces.
ZOYA: If you sign, the forest is gone. Everything your father died for.
ISHA: My brother is tied to a chair, Zoya.
ZOYA: Your father would want you to fight.
ISHA: My father is dead. Nikhil isn't. Not yet.
She signs the last page.
MAYA: (quietly) Thank you.
Isha's phone buzzes.
ON SCREEN: Unknown number: "Old toll naka on the Kufri road. Noon. Bring the papers."
ZOYA: At least take the police.
ISHA: They said no police.
The doors open. Inspector Rawat walks in, cap under his arm.
RAWAT: Miss Sood. I heard about your brother. Mrs. Sood called me.
Isha looks at Maya. Maya nods.
RAWAT: I'll come with you. Plain clothes. Nobody will know.
ISHA: (hesitating) Thank you, Inspector.

INT. ISHA'S CAR, KUFRI ROAD - DAY
Rawat drives. Pine trees blur past. Isha holds the papers on her lap.
RAWAT: Your father and I were in school together. He was the clever one. I was the one who got caught.
ISHA: And now you catch people.
RAWAT: Somebody has to. Don't worry, beta. In an hour, your brother will be home.
ISHA: How can you be so sure?
RAWAT: Because these men want money, not blood. I know the type.

EXT. OLD TOLL NAKA, KUFRI ROAD - DAY
Fog. An abandoned toll booth. A black SUV waits. Isha walks forward with the papers. Rawat stays by her car.
The SUV's back door opens. Nikhil stumbles out and runs to her. She holds him tight.
NIKHIL: Didi... I'm sorry. I'm so sorry.
ISHA: You're safe. That's all that matters.
A gloved hand takes the papers through the SUV window. The SUV drives off into the fog.
Rawat walks up and puts a hand on Nikhil's shoulder.
RAWAT: It's over now. Go home and rest.
His wallet slips from his jacket and falls open on the road. Isha sees his police ID card.
ON SCREEN: Police ID card: "Inspector Karan Rawat, Shimla Police."
Isha stops breathing.
ISHA: (whispering) Karan. K.
Rawat picks up the wallet and smiles at her.
RAWAT: Something wrong, Miss Sood?
ISHA: Nothing. Just cold.

EPISODE 8: The Voice

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, ISHA'S ROOM - NIGHT
Isha, Zoya, Maya and Nikhil sit on the floor around Dad's note.
ISHA: Not even K. Not Kabir. Karan. Karan Rawat.
MAYA: Rawat has eaten at our table for twenty years.
ZOYA: Who better to steal forest records than the man who guards them?
NIKHIL: (slowly) At the toll naka. When he said "it's over now"...
ISHA: What?
NIKHIL: The voice in Dad's room that night. Calm. Deep. It was the same voice.
Silence.
NIKHIL: There's something else. The pen drive in the clock. I took it.
ISHA: You?
NIKHIL: I saw Kabir hide something in the clock the morning Dad died.
He swallows hard.
NIKHIL: I thought it would show me outside the room. I was scared, Didi. I haven't even watched it.
He pulls it out of his sock and puts it in her palm.
ZOYA: If Rawat is on that footage, it's over for him.
MAYA: Then he will come for it.
ISHA: Then we let him think we don't have it. Nobody says a word outside this room.
Maya takes Isha's hand. It's the first time she has ever done it.
MAYA: I was wrong about you.
ISHA: I was wrong about you too.
ZOYA: I'll call my editor. If anything happens to us, the story still runs.
ISHA: Then call her now.
Zoya steps onto the balcony, phone to her ear. Snow settles on her hair.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - NIGHT
Isha carries a laptop downstairs to play the pen drive. Halfway down, she smells smoke.
ZOYA (O.S.): Fire! The archive room!
Thick black smoke pours from under the archive room door. The fire alarm shrieks.
NIKHIL: The old registers might be in there!
ISHA: That's what he wants. He's burning the proof.
A figure runs in from the front door, coat pulled over his head. It is Kabir.
ISHA: Kabir? They let you out?
KABIR: Bail, this evening. Rawat signed it himself.
He kicks the archive door open and disappears into the fire.

EPISODE 9: Smoke

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, ARCHIVE ROOM - NIGHT
Flames climb the wooden shelves. Kabir pulls a steel trunk from under a desk, coughing.
ISHA (O.S.): Kabir! Get out!
KABIR: Not without this!
He drags the trunk out as a burning shelf collapses behind him.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, LOBBY - NIGHT
Staff throw buckets of water and snow. Fire engine sirens outside. Kabir sits on the floor, his palms red and blistered. Zoya opens the trunk.
Inside are thick, singed land registers, wrapped in plastic.
ZOYA: These are the originals. Look. The forest survey numbers. Your father's notes in the margin.
ISHA: (to Kabir) You could have died.
KABIR: He gave me a home when I had nowhere to go. I owed him one fire.
Isha presses a cold towel into his hands. She holds them a moment too long. Neither of them pulls away.
Inspector Rawat walks in from the cold with the firemen, every inch the concerned officer.
RAWAT: Is everyone safe? An electrical short, they're saying. These old buildings.
ZOYA: (under her breath) Electrical. Of course.
RAWAT: I'll need those registers for the inquiry. Evidence.
MAYA: (stepping in front of the trunk) They're wet, Inspector. Let them dry. You can take them in the morning.
Rawat holds her gaze, then smiles.
RAWAT: In the morning, then.
He walks out. Maya turns to Nikhil.
MAYA: Sit on that trunk. Nobody touches it. Not even the firemen.
NIKHIL: Mom, I'm not a child.
MAYA: Then for once, act like the son of Vijay Sood.
Nikhil sits on the trunk. He doesn't move again.

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, BACK OFFICE - NIGHT
Three in the morning. Isha plugs the pen drive into the laptop. Alone. She clicks play.
ON SCREEN: CCTV, Corridor 2, 14 November, 01:58: A man in a police coat knocks on Room 214, carrying a steel thermos.
The man turns toward the camera. Inspector Rawat.
ON SCREEN: CCTV, 02:40: Rawat leaves Room 214, wiping the thermos with a handkerchief.
Isha covers her mouth.
A floorboard creaks behind her. A calm, deep voice.
RAWAT: You shouldn't have watched that, Isha.

EPISODE 10: Not Even K

INT. PINECREST HOTEL, BACK OFFICE - CONTINUOUS
Rawat stands in the doorway, his service pistol in his hand. Calm. Almost sad.
RAWAT: Close the laptop. Slowly.
Isha closes it. Her phone lies face down beside it.
ISHA: Why? He called you a friend.
RAWAT: He was a friend. Then he found a few old registers and became a saint.
ISHA: You poisoned him.
RAWAT: I made him tea. Ginger, the way he liked it. He drank it while telling me he was going to the press.
ISHA: And Kabir's locker?
RAWAT: A case needs someone to blame. Kabir was convenient. So was your stepmother's fear.
ISHA: And the fire?
RAWAT: Old wiring.
He steps closer.
RAWAT: Now give me the pen drive. Or this hotel has another heart attack.
Isha slides the pen drive across the desk.
ISHA: Take it. It's a copy.
Rawat's eyes flick to her phone. She turns it over. A live call to Zoya. Recording.
ZOYA (V.O.): (from the phone) Every word, Inspector. And the SP heard all of it.
Rawat raises the gun. Kabir crashes through the side door and slams into him. The gun skids across the floor. Nikhil kicks it away.
Blue lights flood the windows. Maya walks in with the SP and four officers.
MAYA: Your file is closed, Inspector.
The officers cuff Rawat and lead him out into the snow.

EXT. PINECREST HOTEL, GARDEN - DAWN
The snow has stopped. Smoke still curls from the archive window. Isha stands with Kabir, Maya and Nikhil. Zoya walks up with a singed envelope.
ZOYA: This was inside the steel trunk. It's addressed to you.
Isha opens it. A second will, dated 13 November.
ON SCREEN: Will in Dad's handwriting: "Half of the Pinecrest goes to Isha. The other half goes to my daughter, Zoya."
Isha looks up slowly.
ISHA: Your daughter?
ZOYA: I came here to tell you, not to take anything. I just didn't know how.
ISHA: You're... my sister?
ZOYA: He found me six months ago. I'm not just his source, Isha. I'm his daughter.
