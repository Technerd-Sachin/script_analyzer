Title: The Pitch

EXT. AGENCY OFFICE, TERRACE - NIGHT
City lights below. Music from the office Diwali party drifts up the stairs. VIKRAM (35) leans on the railing and lights a cigarette.
KARAN (29) steps out and joins him.
VIKRAM: Want one?
KARAN: I quit last year.
VIKRAM: Everybody quits. Nobody stays quit.
Karan takes a cigarette from the pack. Vikram lights it for him. They smoke in silence.
KARAN: They're announcing the creative head tonight.
VIKRAM: I know.
KARAN: It's you or me.
VIKRAM: It was always going to be you or me.
Vikram blows out a long line of smoke and stubs the cigarette on the railing.
VIKRAM: Come on. Naina's waiting.

INT. AGENCY OFFICE, PARTY HALL - NIGHT
Fairy lights, loud music. A bartender pours whisky into a row of glasses. NAINA (40), the agency head, raises hers.
NAINA: To the best year this agency has ever had!
KARAN: Cheers!
Karan downs his whisky in one go and reaches for another. Vikram sips his slowly, watching him.
VIKRAM: Slow down.
KARAN: You slow down. That's your fourth.
NAINA: Karan. Vikram. My cabin. Bring your drinks.

INT. AGENCY OFFICE, NAINA'S CABIN - NIGHT
Naina pours three fresh whiskies from a bottle on her desk. She hands them out.
NAINA: The client loved the river campaign. They want the person who wrote it to lead the account.
KARAN: (smiling) That's good news.
NAINA: Vikram, the job is yours. Congratulations.
Karan's smile freezes. He puts his glass down.
KARAN: The river campaign was my idea.
VIKRAM: We built it together.
KARAN: I wrote it at two in the morning and showed it to you at nine.
NAINA: Vikram presented it. The deck has his name on it.
KARAN: Of course it does.
He walks out. Naina looks at Vikram. He looks at his whisky.

EXT. AGENCY OFFICE, TERRACE - NIGHT
Karan lights another cigarette with shaking hands. He takes a long drag. Vikram follows him out.
VIKRAM: Karan, listen. I'll make you my deputy. Same money.
KARAN: You stole my work, and now you want to pay me for it.
VIKRAM: It's not stealing. It's the business.
Karan exhales smoke into the cold air. He laughs softly.
KARAN: Then you won't mind if I tell Naina where you were the night the Mehra account money disappeared.
Vikram's glass slips from his hand and shatters on the floor.
