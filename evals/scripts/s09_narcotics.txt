Title: The Last Party

EXT. FARMHOUSE, GARDEN - NIGHT
Bass thumps from speakers. College students dance under string lights. ROHAN (21) and his best friend DEV (22) sit on the grass with TANYA (21).
Dev pulls out a clay chillum and a small lump of charas. He packs it and lights it.
DEV: Semester's over, bhai. Tonight we forget everything.
He takes a long hit and passes the chillum to Rohan. Rohan smokes it, coughing.
TANYA: Rohan, you have your placement interview on Monday.
ROHAN: Monday is three days away, Tanya. Relax.
TANYA: You said that last month too.
Dev laughs and takes the chillum back.
DEV: Don't listen to her. Come inside. I have something better.

INT. FARMHOUSE, BATHROOM - NIGHT
Dev locks the door. He takes out a small plastic packet of white powder and taps a line of cocaine onto the marble counter.
ROHAN: Is that...?
DEV: Just once. Everybody in Delhi does it.
Dev rolls a currency note and snorts the line. He shuts his eyes and grins.
He taps out another line and holds out the note to Rohan.
Rohan hesitates. Then he bends over the counter and snorts it.
ROHAN: (laughing) My face is numb.
DEV: That means it's working.

INT. FARMHOUSE, PARTY HALL - NIGHT
Rohan dances wildly, sweating, his eyes wide. Tanya grabs his arm.
TANYA: Your heart is racing. I can feel it through your shirt.
ROHAN: I've never felt this good.
Suddenly he clutches his chest. His legs give way. He falls hard on the floor and starts shaking.
TANYA: Rohan! Rohan! Somebody call an ambulance!
Dev backs away into the crowd and disappears.

INT. CITY HOSPITAL, ICU - NIGHT
Monitors beep. Rohan lies on a bed, an oxygen mask on his face. Tanya sits beside him, her eyes red. A DOCTOR checks his chart.
DOCTOR: His heart stopped for forty seconds. He is lucky to be alive.
TANYA: Will he be okay?
DOCTOR: The next few hours will tell us.
INSPECTOR MALIK (45) enters with a constable. He holds up the empty plastic packet in an evidence bag.
MALIK: This was in his pocket. The same batch sent three other students to hospitals tonight.
TANYA: Three?
MALIK: One of them didn't make it.
Rohan opens his eyes. He pulls down the oxygen mask.
ROHAN: (weak) Where's Dev?
MALIK: Your friend Dev? We picked him up an hour ago, with fifty more packets in his car.
Tanya stares at Rohan.
MALIK: He told us everything. He says you were the one who sold it to him.
