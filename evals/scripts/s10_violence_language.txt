Title: Kasai Gali

INT. MEAT MARKET, BACK ROOM - NIGHT
A tube light flickers. Goat carcasses hang from steel hooks. A drain in the floor. MUNNA (30) is pushed in and thrown against the wall.
BALLI (42), a loan shark's enforcer, follows him in. His sidekick PAPPU (25) shuts the door.
BALLI: Three months, Munna. Three fucking months.
MUNNA: Balli bhai, I swear, next week. My mother is in hospital.
BALLI: Your mother, your sister, your whole bhenchod family is always in hospital.
PAPPU: Every month the same story, saala chutiya.
MUNNA: I have twenty thousand. Take it. Please.
Munna holds out a crumpled bundle of notes. Balli knocks it out of his hand.
BALLI: Twenty thousand? You owe four lakh, madarchod!
He punches Munna in the stomach. Munna folds. Balli drives a knee into his face. Munna's nose cracks. Blood pours down his chin.
MUNNA: (spitting blood) Bhai... please...
BALLI: Pappu. Give me the knife.
Pappu hands him a butcher's knife from the table.
BALLI: Let's see what your blood is worth, you fucking bastard.
Balli slashes Munna's forearm. The skin opens to the bone. Blood sprays across the white tiles.
MUNNA: (screaming) Aaah! Stop! Stop, you harami!
PAPPU: (laughing) Listen to him squeal, bhenchod.
Balli raises the knife again. Munna grabs a meat cleaver from the hook rail and swings blindly.
The cleaver splits Balli's cheek open. Balli staggers back, howling, his hand pressed to the gaping wound.
BALLI: You motherfucker! I'll kill you!
Munna lunges and buries the butcher's knife in Balli's belly. He twists it. Balli's eyes go wide. Blood gushes over Munna's fingers and spatters the floor.
Balli drops to his knees, gurgling, blood bubbling from his mouth. He falls face down. A dark pool spreads toward the drain.
Pappu stares at the body, frozen.
PAPPU: What the fuck have you done? You killed Balli bhai!
MUNNA: (panting) Open the door, Pappu. Or you're next, chutiye.
Pappu fumbles with the bolt. His hands shake.
PAPPU: Sethji will cut you into pieces. You know that, right?
MUNNA: Let him fucking try.
Munna's phone rings in his pocket. Blood smears across the screen as he pulls it out.
ON SCREEN: Incoming call: "SETHJI"
Munna stares at the name. He wipes the blood off with his thumb and answers.
MUNNA: (into phone) Haan.
SETHJI (V.O.): Balli is dead? Good. I sent him to die, Munna. Now you work for me.
