Title: Nana's Radio

INT. MEHTA HOUSE, LIVING ROOM - MORNING
Sunlight through lace curtains. An old wooden radio sits on a shelf, polished like a trophy. AARAV (12) dusts it carefully, humming.
His father SUNIL (42) calls from the kitchen.
SUNIL (O.S.): Aarav! Did you wrap Nana's gift?
AARAV: Almost, Papa!
Aarav turns the radio's knob just to see. It crackles. He tries to lift it closer. It slips from his hands and hits the floor. The dial cracks.
AARAV: (whispering) Oh no. No, no, no.
He shoves the radio behind the sofa cushions and sits on them just as Sunil walks in.
SUNIL: What was that noise?
AARAV: Nothing. A book fell.
Sunil narrows his eyes, then smiles.
SUNIL: Nana will be home at six. Tonight is his eightieth birthday. Everything has to be perfect.
AARAV: Perfect. Yes.

INT. MEHTA HOUSE, KITCHEN - DAY
Aarav's mother PRIYA (40) frosts a cake. Aarav's sister MEERA (16) writes "Happy 80th, Nana" in icing.
MEERA: You look like you swallowed a frog.
AARAV: I'm fine.
MEERA: You're never this quiet. What did you break?
AARAV: Why do you think I broke something?
MEERA: Because you only say "I'm fine" when you broke something.
Priya laughs. Aarav doesn't.

INT. MEHTA HOUSE, LIVING ROOM - EVENING
Balloons and paper lanterns. NANA (80) walks in on his cane. Everyone cheers.
NANA: Eighty years! I feel twenty-five. Maybe thirty.
He goes straight to the shelf. It is empty.
NANA: Where is my radio?
Silence. Everyone looks at each other.
SUNIL: Aarav was dusting it this morning.
All eyes turn to Aarav.
AARAV: I... I don't know.
Sunil's face hardens.
SUNIL: Aarav. Look at me. Where is Nana's radio?
Aarav's lip trembles. He stands, lifts the sofa cushion and takes out the radio. The dial is cracked.
AARAV: I dropped it. I'm sorry, Nana. I hid it because I didn't want to spoil your birthday.
SUNIL: Hiding it is worse than breaking it, beta.
AARAV: I know. I'm sorry, Papa.
Nana takes the radio in his hands and turns it gently.
NANA: Do you know who dropped this radio the first time?
AARAV: Who?
NANA: Your Papa. When he was twelve. Right on the same spot.
Sunil looks at the floor. Priya and Meera burst out laughing.
SUNIL: (smiling) He told on me in nineteen ninety-four, and he's still telling on me now.
NANA: And I fixed it then. Aarav, tomorrow you and I will fix it together.
Aarav hugs Nana tight. Nana turns the knob. The radio crackles, then plays an old song.
NANA: See? Some things only crack. They don't break.
