Title: Pehla Karva Chauth

INT. DHILLON HAVELI, KITCHEN - DAWN
Still dark outside. PREET (24), a new bride with fresh chooda on her wrists, eats sargi with her mother-in-law BEEJI (62). Mathri, pheniyan, a glass of milk.
BEEJI: Kha le, puttar. Aaj suraj se chaand tak, paani ki ek boond bhi nahi.
PREET: Beeji, mera pehla Karva Chauth hai aur aaj hi Bunty ka sangeet bhi. Main nachungi kaise bhookhi?
BEEJI: Isi liye toh mehendi wale haath sabse sundar lagte hain. Bhookh mein bhi.
PREET: Harry ne promise kiya hai, chaand nikalne se pehle aa jayega.
BEEJI: Mere Harry ka promise? Rab rakha.

EXT. DHILLON HAVELI, COURTYARD - NIGHT
Fairy lights. A dhol. The sangeet is in full swing. Harry's elder brother JASSI (32) tries to dance bhangra and stumbles. His cousin DOLLY (22) shrieks with laughter.
DOLLY: Naach na jaane aangan tedha, Pai ji!
JASSI: Oye, aangan sach mein tedha hai! Contractor ne paise khaaye!
Everyone laughs. The dhol player shouts.
DOLLY: Shava shava! Balle balle!
PREET: (checking her phone) Harry ka phone switch off aa raha hai.
DOLLY: Bhabhi, jaggo nikalne wali hai. Chalo, gaagar uthao!
The women lift a brass gaagar lit with diyas onto Preet's head. The jaggo procession moves through the lane, singing, banging steel plates.
DOLLY: Jaggo aayi aa! Jaagde raho!

INT. DHILLON HAVELI, ROOF - NIGHT
Thali with a diya, a karva of water, and a chhanni. The women wait for the moon. Preet's lips are dry. Beeji holds her hand.
BEEJI: Chaand nikalne wala hai. Harry kahan hai?
PREET: Pata nahi, Beeji. Highway pe jam hai, last message mein likha tha.
JASSI: Fikar na kar, Preet. Video call pe chhanni se dekh lena.
BEEJI: (sharp) Video call pe vrat nahi khulta, Jassi. Mard saamne hona chahiye.
Below, in the kitchen, the smell of sarson da saag and makki di roti rises. Preet hasn't eaten since dawn.
DOLLY: (pointing) Chaand! Chaand aa gaya!
The full moon rises over the rooftops. All the women lift their chhannis. Preet lifts hers, and sees only the empty stairs.
PREET: Beeji, ek baar aur try karti hoon.
She calls. This time the phone rings. Someone answers.
PREET: (relieved) Harry! Kahan ho tum? Chaand nikal aaya hai.
WOMAN (V.O.): Harry naha raha hai. Aap kaun bol rahi hain?
Preet slowly lowers the chhanni. The diya on her thali flickers out.
