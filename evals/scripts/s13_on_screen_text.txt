Title: Seen at 2:14

INT. MEHER'S FLAT, BEDROOM - NIGHT
MEHER (25) lies in bed, half asleep, her phone glowing on the pillow.
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara, 11:02 pm: "Date is going great!! Don't wait up."
Meher smiles and sends a heart. She turns off the lamp.
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara, 12:47 am: "Actually... something feels off."
Meher sits up.
MEHER: (typing) Off how? Where are you?
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara, 1:15 am: "He locked the car doors. He won't tell me where we're going."
MEHER: (typing) Call me. Right now.
ON SCREEN: Tara shared her live location. "Last updated 1:16 am, Old Mill Road."
Meher calls. It doesn't connect.
ON SCREEN: Call failed: "The number you are calling is switched off."
MEHER: Come on, Tara. Come on.
She grabs her jacket and keys.

EXT. CITY STREETS - NIGHT
Meher rides through empty streets, phone clipped to the handlebar, the map glowing.
ON SCREEN: Navigation: "Old Mill Road. 4 km. Road closed ahead."
She swerves around a barricade.
ON SCREEN: Road sign: "OLD MILL ROAD. NO ENTRY AFTER 10 PM."

EXT. ABANDONED TEXTILE MILL - NIGHT
A rusted gate. A car parked inside, doors open, engine ticking as it cools. No one in it.
ON SCREEN: Faded board on the gate: "SHARMA TEXTILE MILL. CLOSED SINCE 1998. TRESPASSERS WILL BE PROSECUTED."
MEHER: (calling out) Tara? Tara!
Her own voice echoes back. Her phone buzzes.
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara: "Tara is typing..."
Meher stares at the screen. The dots appear and disappear. Appear and disappear.
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara, 2:13 am: "Why did you come, Meher?"
MEHER: (typing, shaking) Where are you? I'm at the gate.
ON SCREEN: Message status: "Seen 2:14 am."
A long silence. Then one more notification.
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara, 2:14 am: "I'm not the one who sent you those messages."
Meher's breath catches. Behind her, the car's headlights switch on.
ON SCREEN: Chat from Tara, 2:15 am: "Turn around."
