Title: Second Show

EPISODE 1: The Visitor

INT. NAIK CABLE NETWORK, OFFICE - NIGHT
Sonapur, a hill town. Stacks of DVDs. RAGHU NAIK (45), a cable TV operator who left school after class four, is lost in an old crime thriller.
RAGHU: (to the screen) Wrong. The police always catch the one who changes his story.
His wife SAVITA (40) calls on his mobile.
SAVITA (V.O.): Film over? Your daughters are waiting for dinner.
RAGHU: Second show, Savita. Ten minutes.

INT. NAIK HOUSE, BEDROOM - NIGHT
Rain. Raghu is still at the office. MEENU (17), his elder daughter, freezes at the window. VARUN (19) stands outside, smiling.
VARUN: (through the window) Open the door, Meenu. You blocked my number. That was rude.
MEENU: Go away, Varun. I told you at the camp. I'm not interested.
Savita comes in.
SAVITA: Who is this boy?
VARUN: The son of IG Radhika Menon, aunty. Ask anyone. Nobody says no to me.
He climbs in through the back door. He grabs Meenu's wrist. Savita pulls him off. He shoves Savita against the wall.
Meenu picks up an iron rod from beside the door and swings it. It strikes Varun's head. He drops. He does not move.
SAVITA: (kneeling) Varun? Varun!
MEENU: (whispering) Mummy... is he...?
SAVITA: He's not breathing.
At the bedroom door, CHINKI (10), the youngest, stands watching. Savita and Meenu drag the body out into the rain, toward the compost pit behind the house.

EPISODE 2: The Alibi

INT. NAIK HOUSE, KITCHEN - DAWN
Raghu returns. Savita, shaking, tells him everything. Raghu listens without a word.
RAGHU: Where is he now?
SAVITA: In the compost pit. Chinki saw us.
RAGHU: And his phone? His car?
MEENU: The car is near the gate. The phone is in his pocket.
Raghu closes his eyes. He thinks like a man rewinding a film.
RAGHU: Listen carefully. Two days ago, all four of us went to Bhavanpur.
SAVITA: We were never in Bhavanpur.
RAGHU: We are now. The satsang. A film. Dinner at Hotel Sagar. A night at Lodge Shanti.
RAGHU: I have the tickets and the bills. We all tell the same story. Every time. Word for word.
That night, Raghu drops Varun's phone into a truck headed out of the state. He pushes Varun's car into the flooded quarry.

INT. SONAPUR POLICE STATION - DAY
IG RADHIKA MENON (48) stares at a missing-person poster of her son. SUB-INSPECTOR PATIL (40), who has a grudge against Raghu, stands beside her.
PATIL: The cable fellow, madam. His car was seen near the quarry. I've never trusted him.
RADHIKA: Bring the whole family in.

EPISODE 3: The Foundation

INT. SONAPUR POLICE STATION, INTERROGATION ROOM - DAY
The family sits apart. Each one repeats the same story, word for word. The satsang, the film, Hotel Sagar, Lodge Shanti.
RADHIKA: They're reciting it like a film script.
PATIL: Then we break the smallest one.
Patil slaps Raghu across the face. Then Savita. Then he grabs Chinki's arm and raises his baton.
CHINKI: (sobbing) Stop! I'll tell! We put him in the pit behind the house!
EXT. NAIK HOUSE, COMPOST PIT - DAY
Constables dig in the rain. Radhika watches, trembling. A shovel strikes something.
They pull out the carcass of a dog.
RADHIKA: (turning to Patil) A dog. You beat a family and terrified a child for a dog.
The town gathers at the gate, furious at the police. Radhika is suspended. Patil is transferred.

INT. NEW SONAPUR POLICE STATION - DAY (WEEKS LATER)
A freshly built police station. The paint is new. Raghu signs the visitor register at the front desk.
On his way out, he stops on the new floor and looks down at it for a long time.
EXT. SONAPUR POLICE STATION, CONSTRUCTION SITE - NIGHT (FLASHBACK)
The night after Varun's death. The station is still under construction. Raghu digs alone in the rain, under the foundation.
RAGHU (V.O.): Every film has a secret the audience never sees. Mine is under your feet.
