Title: The Red Scarf

INT. RIYA'S FLAT - EVENING
RIYA (29) stands at the mirror and wraps a red scarf around her neck. It was a gift from her brother Sameer, before the accident.
Her phone buzzes.
ON SCREEN: Message from Kabir: "Moonlight Cafe. 7 pm. Please come."
RIYA: (to herself) Five years of silence, and now he wants coffee.
Her flatmate NISHA (28) leans in the doorway.
NISHA: Kabir? The Kabir? You're actually going?
RIYA: He left the day after Sameer's funeral. No call. No letter. It's been five years.
NISHA: Then don't go.
RIA: I have to. He was driving that night. I need to hear him say it.

INT. MOONLIGHT CAFE - NIGHT
The cafe is half empty. Soft music. KABIR (31) sits at a corner table, a cup of black coffee going cold. He stands when Riya enters.
KABIR: You came.
RIYA: I came for answers. Not for coffee.
She sits. She keeps the red scarf on, like armour.
KABIR: I've rehearsed this a thousand times. For three years, Riya. Three years I waited to say sorry.
RIYA: Sorry for what? Leaving? Or killing my brother?
KABIR: I wasn't driving.
RIYA: You were behind the wheel when they pulled you out.
KABIR: Sameer was driving. After the crash, he begged me to switch seats before the police came. He was drunk, Riya.
RIA: You're lying. You're blaming him because he can't answer back.
KABIR: He made me promise never to tell you. I kept that promise until he started coming in my dreams.
Riya's hands tremble. She pulls at her blue scarf.
KABIR: I chose this temple because you used to pray here every Monday. I thought you'd feel safe.
The temple bell rings. Riya looks at him, confused, then angry.
RIYA: Safe? You want me to feel safe while you blame a dead boy?
KABIR: There's a witness. The truck driver. He saw Sameer at the wheel.
RIYA: Then why didn't he say it five years ago?
KABIR: Because someone paid him not to.
RIYA: Who?
Kabir slides a photo across the table. Riya picks it up and goes white.
RIYA: That's... my father.
