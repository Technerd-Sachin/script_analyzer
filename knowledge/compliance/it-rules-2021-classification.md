IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021, Code of Ethics for online curated content (OTT). Quotes are from the official MeitY consolidated text (updated 6.4.2023), fetched 2026-10-19.

**Bands.** U (all ages), U/A 7+, U/A 13+, U/A 16+ (each viewable below that age with parental guidance), A (restricted to adults).

**Display.** The publisher shall "prominently display the classification rating specific to each content or programme together with a content descriptor informing the user about the nature of the content, and advising on viewer discretion (if applicable) at the beginning of every programme".

**Access control.** Content "classified as U/A 13+ or higher shall ensure that access control mechanisms, including parental locks, are made available". For A, the publisher "shall implement a reliable age verification mechanism for viewership of such content."

**Schedule Part I, general factors** (apply at every level): (a) context, (b) theme, judged mainly on treatment, (c) tone and impact, judged on the work in its entirety, (d) target audience.

**Schedule Part II, issue categories:** (a) discrimination; (b) psychotropic substances, liquor, smoking and tobacco; (c) imitable behaviour (weapons, dangerous acts including suicide and self-harm that children may copy, sexual innuendo in songs); (d) language; (e) nudity; (f) sex; (g) violence.

- Nudity: "Nudity with a sexual context will receive a higher classification of “A”."
- Sex: "The classification of content in various ratings from U/A 16+ to “A” shall depend upon the portrayal of non-explicit (implicit) to explicit depiction of sexual behaviour."

**Note.** The Code of Ethics also lists "Horror" as a basis for classification. The house matrix has no horror category; treat horror under theme.

This is an estimate to support editors, not a certification or legal advice.
