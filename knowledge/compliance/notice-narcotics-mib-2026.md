Source reachable on 2026-10-19. The PDF is a scan; its text was extracted and checked against the page image. The warning text above is verbatim, including the original grammar ("Guarantees").

Advisory text, para 4: display the warning "during scenes depicting consumption/use of narcotic drugs in a clear, legible and prominent manner". Para 5: font, colour and size easily readable, "for a duration sufficient to ensure its effective visibility and communication". Para 6: applies to "all content published/released on the platform on or after the date of issue of advisory".

It continues the MIB advisory of 26 November 2024, which asked OTT platforms to adhere to the NDPS Act 1985, classify drug content properly, and add warnings and disclaimers to content depicting drug use.

Trigger only on depicted consumption or use of narcotic drugs. References, dealing without use, and alcohol do not trigger this notice (classification still applies).
