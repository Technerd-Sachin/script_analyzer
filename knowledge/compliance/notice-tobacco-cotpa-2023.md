Trigger: any scene that shows tobacco products or their use (cigarette, beedi, gutka, khaini, hookah, vape and similar). Report the triggering scenes and line IDs.

The required static warning text is one of "Tobacco causes cancer" or "Tobacco kills", in the programme's language. Requirements summarize the Khaitan & Co note on the Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products (COTPA) amendment rules, 2023. Not verified against the official gazette text.

A mention of tobacco without depiction triggers classification only (substances, mild), not this notice.
