---
type: craft_reference
kind: cliffhanger
items:
  - id: revelation
    name: Revelation
    description: "A hidden fact comes out in the last seconds and changes how the viewer reads earlier scenes."
    example: "NURSE: Jis bachche ko aap das saal se paal rahe hain, woh aapka nahi hai."
  - id: reversal
    name: Reversal
    description: "The situation flips: the winner loses, the ally turns out to be the enemy, or the plan backfires."
    example: "VIKRAM: (smiling, to the inspector) Gun meri nahi hai, sir. Yeh sab Meera ka plan tha."
  - id: threat
    name: Threat
    description: "A clear danger to a character is set up and left unresolved, so the viewer must return for the outcome."
    example: "PHONE SCREEN: Kal subah 9 baje tak paise nahi aaye, toh Pihu school se ghar nahi aayegi."
  - id: unanswered_question
    name: Unanswered question
    description: "The episode ends on a direct question or mystery that the next episode promises to answer."
    example: "MEERA: Agar Aditya mar chuka hai, toh yeh message kisne bheja?"
  - id: emotional_gut_punch
    name: Emotional gut punch
    description: "End on a moment of loss, rejection or sacrifice that hits hard and leaves the viewer needing relief."
    example: "MAA: Tu meri beti nahi hai. Aaj se is ghar ke darwaze tere liye band hain."
  - id: arrival
    name: Arrival
    description: "A character whose entry changes everything appears in the final shot."
    example: "The mandap doors open. ADITYA, declared dead five years ago, walks in."
---
Ending techniques for the last 1-3 lines of an episode. Cut at the peak, before the answer. Each alternative ending must use a different technique and pay off a setup line.
