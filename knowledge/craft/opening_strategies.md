---
type: craft_reference
kind: opening
items:
  - id: in_medias_res
    name: In medias res
    description: "Start in the middle of a scene already under pressure, with no setup. The viewer catches up while the action runs."
    example: "RIYA: (pinned against the car door) Chaabi de do, warna main chillaungi!"
  - id: mystery_question
    name: Mystery question
    description: "The first line raises a specific question the viewer needs answered, and the episode withholds the answer."
    example: "MEERA: Agar kal subah tak main wapas na aaun, toh locker number 47 kholna."
  - id: shock_reveal
    name: Shock reveal
    description: "Open on a revelation that upends a relationship or a known fact, then play the fallout."
    example: "DOCTOR: DNA report ke hisaab se, Vikram aapka beta nahi hai."
  - id: flash_forward
    name: Flash forward
    description: "Show a high-stakes moment from later in the story first, then cut back to how it began."
    example: "SUPER: 72 ghante baad. KAVYA, in a torn bridal lehenga, hands a bloodied knife to the police."
  - id: conflict_first_line
    name: Conflict in the first line
    description: "The first spoken line is a direct clash between two characters with opposing wants."
    example: "SAAS: Is ghar mein ya toh tum rahogi, ya meri izzat."
  - id: voiceover_hook
    name: Voice-over hook
    description: "A character's voice-over states a provocative fact or confession over the opening image."
    example: "ANANYA (V.O.): Meri shaadi ek contract thi. Aur aaj uski expiry date hai."
---
Opening strategies for the first 3 lines (about 5 seconds) of a vertical episode. Each alternative opening must use a different strategy.
