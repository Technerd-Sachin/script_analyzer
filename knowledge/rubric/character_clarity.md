With 1-2 minutes per episode, each lead must be readable from one line or action. Check for a distinct voice, a visible want, and a past hurt (wound) that drives them. Familiar archetypes (scheming in-law, loyal friend) are fine when specific. Penalize leads who sound the same or explain their feelings.
