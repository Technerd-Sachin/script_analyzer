Micro-drama lines are short, one or two sentences. Penalize characters telling each other facts they both know, and long speeches. Credit subtext, sharp retorts, and Hinglish that sounds natural for the speaker. Heightened, melodramatic lines are a genre convention: judge them on punch and clarity, not restraint.
