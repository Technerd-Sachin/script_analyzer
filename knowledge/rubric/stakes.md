Ask what the lead loses on failure: a marriage, a child, a home, family honour, freedom, a life. The viewer must grasp it inside the episode, ideally in the first 30 seconds. Izzat and family reputation are real stakes in Indian drama. Abstract stakes (happiness, the future) score 2-3 unless tied to a concrete loss.
