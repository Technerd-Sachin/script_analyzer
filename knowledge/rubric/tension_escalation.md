Track pressure exchange by exchange. In micro-drama every 10-20 seconds needs a new turn: a reveal, threat, refusal or reversal. Penalize the same argument restated and an early answer to the main question. For multi-episode scripts, judge the rise within and across episodes. Cite lines where pressure rises or stalls.
